## Technical Data & Operating Parameters

### 1. Electrical Ratings
- **Rated Power**: 75 kW / 100 HP
- **Rated Voltage**: 460 VAC ±10%, 3-Phase, 60 Hz
- **Full Load Current (FLC)**: 118 Amperes @ 460 V
- **Locked Rotor Current (LRC)**: 710 Amperes (6.0 x FLC)
- **Power Factor**: 0.88 at 100% Load
- **Insulation Class**: Class H (180°C rated), Class B temperature rise (80°C max rise)
- **Service Factor**: 1.15 continuous duty

### 2. Protection Devices & Settings
- **Overload Relay Model**: OL-1 Thermal Bimetallic Relay (Setting Range: 100 A - 150 A)
- **Default Overload Trip Current**: 147.5 A (125% of FLC)
- **Overload Reset Mode**: Manual Reset via Blue Pushbutton on Panel MB-1
- **Phase Protection Relay (PPR-2)**: Trips on Phase Loss or Phase Imbalance > 2.5%
- **Stator Thermal Switches (TS1/TS2)**: Normally Closed NC, Opens at 130°C ± 5°C, Auto-resets at 90°C ± 5°C

### 3. Mechanical & Physical Specifications
- **Frame Size**: NEMA 405T / IEC 250M
- **Enclosure Protection**: IP66 Weatherproof / Dust-tight
- **Nominal Speed**: 1780 RPM
- **Drive Shaft Diameter**: 60.0 mm (+0.000 / -0.013 mm)
- **Drive End (DE) Bearing**: 6314-C3 Deep Groove Ball Bearing
- **Non-Drive End (NDE) Bearing**: 6212-C3 Deep Groove Ball Bearing
- **Lubricant**: Mobilith SHC 220 Synthetic Grease (15g DE / 10g NDE)
- **Total Net Weight**: 680 kg (1499 lbs)
